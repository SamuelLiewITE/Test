Nomination Categories & Evaluation Standards

Criteria established for the ITE College West Student Awards Committee.

★ Lee Kuan Yew Model Student Award

The premier institutional accolade recognizing the pinnacle of all-round achievement.

  • Exceptional academic standing (Recommended cGPA ≥ 3.80).
  • Grade A in Co-Curricular Activities (CCA).
  • Significant leadership portfolio (Council, ExCo, or National youth bodies).
  • Passionate engagement in community service (≥ 40 VIA hours).
  • Exemplifies the values of Integrity, Teamwork, and Excellence.

⚙ Academic & Technical Excellence Award

Celebrating mastery in practical craft, trade skills, and intellectual vigor.

  • Top 5% rank in technical coursework and practical competencies.
  • Participation or medals in WorldSkills Singapore, hackathons, or national skills challenges.
  • Exemplary attendance rate (≥ 95%) and classroom discipline.
  • Outstanding Industry Attachment (internship) employer appraisal.

❤ Exemplary Leadership & Service Award

Celebrating the "Hearts-on" spirit and servant leadership across the campus.

  • Active leadership tenure in Student Council or college CCAs.
  • Demonstrated initiative in organizing campus-wide or community-based service projects.
  • Positive influence and empathetic mentorship to fellow classmates.

⚡ Innovation & Enterprise Award

Recognizing inventive problem-solvers and student innovators.

  • Development of ingenious prototypes, apps, or business ideas.
  • Demonstrated commercial or campus utility solving real-world challenges.
  • Winner or finalist in enterprise pitching or design thinking showcases.
